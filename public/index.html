How to Play Capsa Banting · Big Two

🎯
GoalBe the first to play all 13 of your cards.
🃏
Your turnBeat the pile with the same number of cards — or pass. When everyone else passes, the last player to play leads anything.
🥇
First moveThe very first play must include the lowest dealt card.
⏱️
Timer30 seconds per turn — then you auto-pass, or auto-play your lowest card when leading.
